# PayForm

> PayForm is a no-code payment form builder for accepting one-time and recurring credit-card payments without writing code. Creators and small businesses design customizable checkout forms, run recurring billing with trials and setup fees, and deliver digital content through a members-only area. It supports 50+ currencies and 30+ languages, connects to Stripe and PayPal, and offers Zapier automation and webhooks for custom integrations.
